Privacy Policy – Dots and Boxes

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Developer: Abhishek

1. Introduction

Dots and Boxes is an offline-first puzzle game. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores game-related information locally on your device, which may include:

  • Game scores and records
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Selected light or dark theme
  • Game progress and tutorial preferences

This information is stored using local device storage and is not transmitted to a developer-operated server.

3. Internet Access and Google Fonts

The game can be played without an internet connection. When an internet connection is available, the app may request fonts from Google Fonts. This request may expose technical information, such as your IP address and device request details, to Google.

Google's privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

If the font request fails or the device is offline, the app uses locally available fallback fonts.

4. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell or share locally stored game data with third parties. The optional Google Fonts request is described above.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of the app does not include advertising SDKs or analytics services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game information remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. This may permanently erase locally stored game information.

7. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users through a developer-operated account or server.

8. Security

Game data is stored locally on your device. Please use your device's security features to protect access to that information.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available on this webpage.
